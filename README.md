#  MineGuard AI

### AI-Powered Early Lung-Risk Detection & Prevention Platform for Mine Workers

**MineGuard AI** is a mobile-first AI-powered preventive health platform designed to identify early respiratory risk patterns among mine workers exposed to coal dust, silica, and other hazardous airborne particles.

> **Detect Risk Earlier. Protect Workers Sooner.**

---

## 🌍 Problem

Mine workers can experience prolonged exposure to hazardous airborne particles, increasing their risk of serious respiratory conditions.

The major challenges include:

* Symptoms can develop gradually and remain unnoticed.
* Regular medical screening may be difficult to access.
* Remote mining communities may lack healthcare infrastructure.
* Low literacy can make conventional digital health applications difficult to use.
* Existing approaches are often reactive rather than preventive.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

MineGuard AI provides a simple way for workers to record their daily health indicators using **voice input and icon-based interactions**.

The platform analyses changes in reported symptoms and exposure patterns to generate a **non-diagnostic lung-risk assessment** and early alerts.

The goal is to encourage timely preventive action and professional medical consultation before risks become severe.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎙️ Voice-Based Reporting

Workers can report symptoms through voice instead of relying entirely on text-based forms.

### 🌐 Local-Language Accessibility

Voice interaction can support local languages, helping reduce language and literacy barriers.

### 🧠 AI Risk Scoring

The system analyses indicators such as:

* Cough frequency
* Breathing difficulty
* Fatigue
* Exposure duration
* Long-term symptom trends

### 🚨 Early Risk Alerts

Increasing risk patterns can trigger alerts for workers and designated healthcare stakeholders.

### 📊 Healthcare Dashboard

Aggregated insights can help healthcare providers, NGOs, and safety organisations monitor population-level trends.

### 🔐 Responsible AI

MineGuard AI is designed around:

* User consent
* Data privacy
* Explainable risk assessment
* Human oversight
* Non-diagnostic AI

## 🧑‍⚕️ Important Safety Principle

**MineGuard AI does not diagnose diseases.**

The platform provides risk assessments based on reported patterns and is intended to support preventive action and professional healthcare decisions.

Medical diagnosis and treatment decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.
